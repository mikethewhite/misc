Review my interactions with [John Doe] ([employee #]) over the past 12 months, 
including chats, emails, meetings, meeting transcripts, and shared documents. 
Summarize what these interactions show about how they work, organized under 
the headings below.

1. Contributions and outcomes: What they delivered or drove, and the effect 
   on their team, the broader business, the firm, and clients. Include scope, 
   ownership, quality, and timeliness.

2. Policy, risk, and controls: How they approached compliance, risk 
   management, change controls, security, and firm standards. Note both 
   adherence to the letter of a requirement and judgment about its intent 
   (e.g., raising risks early, escalating issues, documenting decisions).

3. Collaboration and workplace contribution: How they worked with others, 
   communicated, handled disagreement, included different perspectives, and 
   contributed to a high-standards environment.

4. People leadership (only if they manage others): How they set direction, 
   managed expectations, developed team members, and supported an inclusive 
   team culture.

5. Feedback and growth themes: Any feedback, coaching, or development 
   suggestions previously given to them (by me or others in these materials), 
   and whether there is evidence of follow-through.

For each section:
- Give 2–4 key observations, each backed by specific examples with dates and 
  a reference to the source (thread, meeting, or document).
- Include both strengths and areas where things fell short.
- Distinguish clearly between what is directly evidenced and what is inferred.
- If evidence for a section is thin, say so rather than filling the gap.

End with a short list of the 3 most consistent characteristics across all 
sections, and any notable change in pattern over the 12 months.
